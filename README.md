EMBERLINE is a wood-fired kitchen rush: a complete, original cooking game that runs offline in a desktop browser from its own static files. Read the order tickets, run the line — pantry, chopping boards, grill, fryer, plating counter — and serve every dish at the window before its ticket walks out.

One shift lasts 2½ minutes. Rush hour hits at the one-minute mark and tickets fly in twice as fast. Food left on the grill or in the fryer burns; burnt food goes in the trash. Fast serves earn tips, back-to-back serves build a streak, and perfect serves pay extra.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, or click the floor. Click a station to walk over and use it.
- **Use a station:** E or Space (or click it when standing next to it).
- **Pantry:** take a raw ingredient. **Chop:** raw tomato / lettuce, 2s. **Grill:** patty / mushroom / dough, 4s. **Fryer:** potato, 3s.
- **Plates:** take an empty plate, then build the dish right in your hand — one hand holds the plate, the other fetches ingredients: bun and cheese go straight on, the rest needs chopping, grilling or frying first. You can also set a plate down on the plating counter and build it there. **Serve** at the window when the plate matches an open ticket.
- **Trash:** dump burnt food or a wrong plate. **P:** pause. **R:** cook again after the shift ends.

Six recipes unlock across the shift: Garden Salad, Ember Burger, Golden Fries, Margherita Flatbread, Shroom Melt, and the Ember Platter.
